# ✈️ European Flights Exploratory Data Analysis (2016-2022)

## About The Project
This project performs an in-depth Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on European flights data spanning from 2016 to 2022. The main objective is to uncover patterns, trends, and actionable insights regarding air traffic across various European countries and airports over the years.

## Dataset Overview
The dataset contains detailed records of flights, including:
* Date and Time information (Year, Month, Day of the week, Season).
* Airport details (Codes and Names).
* Country tracking (e.g., Slovakia, Armenia, etc.).
* Flight metrics (Number of departures, arrivals, and total flights).

## Key Objectives of the EDA
* **Trend Analysis:** Understand how flight volumes changed from 2016 to 2022 (including the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic if applicable).
* **Seasonal Variations:** Analyze which seasons and days of the week experience the highest air traffic.
* **Busiest Hubs:** Identify the busiest airports and countries in terms of flight frequency.

## Tools & Libraries Used
* **Python** (Core Language)
* **Pandas & NumPy** (Data Cleaning and Manipulation)
* **Matplotlib & Seaborn** (Data Visualization)
* **Jupyter Notebook** (Interactive Development)
